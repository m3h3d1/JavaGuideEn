At midnight on March 21, 2021, after finishing a frontend feature for a project I was working on, I opened [my GitHub profile](https://github.com/Snailclimb).

To my surprise, while I had not been paying attention for a few days, [JavaGuide](https://github.com/Snailclimb/JavaGuide) had reached 100K stars.

![JavaGuide reaches 100K stars](https://oss.javaguide.cn/github/javaguide/1&e=1643644799&token=kIxbL07-8jAj8w1n4s9zv64FuZZNEATmlU_Vm6zD:zANqh9HQEvvLPm6smyrjvjAt-Ik=.png)

There is not much to boast about. Tutorials are not necessarily as substantial as other projects, and their audience is broad. People may star one because they find it useful, much like bookmarking it. Many excellent frameworks have only a few thousand stars. So star counts alone do not mean much—take them with a grain of salt!

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/github/javaguide/about-the-author/20210323132635635.png)

While maintaining the project, I have also heard comments like: “It's just a Markdown project, not worth much, and an embarrassment to the country!”

Comments like that do not affect me. I'll keep improving JavaGuide and making it better. Many projects from around the world are written entirely in Markdown, including the Awesome collections and job-search and interview guides. Actions speak for themselves!

Collaboration is an important part of open source. If you open-source a project and then stop maintaining it, ignoring issues and pull requests, there is little point in open-sourcing it.

Many of my WeChat followers found me through this project. During my lunch break, I wanted to look back briefly as a way of acknowledging everyone who follows it.

I created JavaGuide while preparing for fall recruitment interviews in my third year of university. I made the **first commit** on **2018-05-07**.

As of today (2021-03-23), the repository has **2,933** commits and **207** contributors.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/github/javaguide/about-the-author/watermark,type_ZmFuZ3poZW5naGVpdGk,shadow_10,text_aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9nLmNzZG4ubmV0L3FxXzM0MzM3Mjcy,size_16,color_FFFFFF,t_70.png)

It has received **511 issues** and **575 pull requests**. All pull requests have been handled; I have not yet had time to address about 15 issues.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/github/javaguide/about-the-author/watermark,type_ZmFuZ3poZW5naGVpdGk,shadow_10,text_aHR0cHM6Ly9ibG9nLmNzZG4ubmV0L3FxXzM0MzM3Mjcy,size_16,color_FFFFFF,t_70-20230309224044850.png)

Issues and pull requests say more about a project's value than its star count.

I will not say much about misleading people into starring a project or buying stars; it is embarrassing. If people find your project useful, they will star it naturally.

**I will continue improving JavaGuide over the coming years.**

**I hope to open-source some useful tools of my own in the future. Onward!**
