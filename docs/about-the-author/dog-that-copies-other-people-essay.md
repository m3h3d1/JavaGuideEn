Plagiarists are so frustrating...

A friend told me that one of my articles had been stolen and reposted on Zhihu to attract traffic, word for word.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/39f223bd8d8240b8b7328f7ab6edbc57~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

And that's not even the most infuriating part.

The person didn't even credit me as the original author at the end of the article...

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/fa47e0752f4b4b57af424114bc6bc558~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

It turns out someone on CSDN had stolen the article, claimed it as original, and then a Zhihu user copied that CSDN post word for word.

It's so absurd you couldn't make it up.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/6f8d281579224b13ad235c28e1d7790e~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

I opened the source link listed by the Zhihu plagiarist. Sure enough, the content was identical and was also marked as original.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/6a6d7b206b6a43ec9b0055a8f47a30be~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

I looked through this CSDN user's posts. They had copied a whole series of my popular answers. Quite the work ethic...

I won't say much more about CSDN. It's a huge dump of articles, with all kinds of uncredited reposts and low-quality paid downloads. The site claims to have the most traffic among Chinese technology sites, but its content practices leave a bad taste. Avoid it if you can.

When I search with Google, I block CSDN altogether. You can install the Chrome extension Personal Blocklist, then add `blog.csdn.net` to the block list.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/be151d93cd024c6e911d1a694212d91c~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

Most of my articles have been copied. My own posts get little traffic, while the people who steal them often get more than I do.

What a world—is this a distortion of human nature or a collapse of morality?

Still, it's fine. I can live without posting on CSDN.

Just look at the articles on CSDN's trending list: ads and hastily stitched-together posts, with little substance.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/cd07efe86af74ea0a07d29236718ddc8~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1-20230717155426403.png)

Of course, there are a few high-quality articles too, including work by bloggers such as Tao Ge, Binghe, and Wei Guan Technology.

There are also many creators on video platforms such as Douyin and Bilibili who turn other people's original work into videos to attract traffic or followers.

The article I mentioned was once turned into a promotional video by a training company to attract students.

![](https://oss.javaguide.cn/p3-juejin/9dda1e36ceff4cbb9b0bf9501b279be5~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-1.png)

As individuals, there isn't much we can do. We can only report each case as we find it...
